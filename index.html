02 / In progress

Ongoing research

SCROLL SIDEWAYS →
Asset pricing · tail risk

Skewness — The anatomy of the rational skewness premium

This replication and extension studies how skewness preferences interact with expected growth and downside risk. Analytic bounds and simulation compare expected returns, expected shortfall, leverage feedback, and investment horizons to characterize when a skewness premium can be consistent with rational preferences.

Quantitative finance · rough volatility

Spectral — Risk under rough volatility

The project studies spectral risk measures for delta-hedged positions under rough volatility. Joint price–variance simulation and short-horizon analysis decompose hedging P&L and compare long- and short-gamma exposures, with bootstrap uncertainty and checks against the model’s theoretical restrictions.

Statistics · nonparametric diagnostics

STAT_test_MFKAN — Conditional model diagnostics

A family of companion studies develops residual-based diagnostics using structured spline and Kolmogorov–Arnold sieves. The framework investigates conditional bias through permutation procedures and states explicitly the exchangeability, approximation, and optimization conditions needed for valid conclusions.

Urban mobility · stochastic networks

BUDAPEST — Markov passenger flows

This project develops a multimodal stochastic representation of Budapest’s public transport network from GTFS schedules. Modal continuous-time Markov chains support passenger-flow simulation and tests of network resilience under stop removal.

Decision theory · sports analytics

DARTS — The tactics threshold

A probabilistic model asks when deliberate aiming in darts ceases to outperform untargeted throws. By smoothing the board’s scoring field with a player-error distribution, the analysis derives a precision threshold below which tactical aiming offers no expected-score advantage.

Computer vision · applied economics

Deep learning — Visual design and automotive value

Frozen image encoders are used to measure novelty, differentiation, and apparent obsolescence in car design. The study examines whether these visual characteristics help explain demand, prices, and depreciation, while carefully separating what the available cross-sectional data can and cannot identify.

Sports economics · organizational decisions

NBA — Draft decisions and sunk costs

Two empirical designs study how draft decisions persist inside NBA franchises: whether a prospect’s playing-time premium changes after the drafting executive leaves, and whether selecting a player earlier than expert rankings suggest predicts later usage. Current results are descriptive and motivate stronger causal designs.

Health economics · public policy

ROTAVIRUS — Evidence-based regional allocation

A reproducible data infrastructure brings together observed rotavirus burden, healthcare costs, and prevention needs to evaluate need-based public support. The framework preserves the distinction between observed, estimated, and assumed quantities and respects the geographic resolution supported by official data.

Polling · Bayesian forecasting

US ELECTION — State polls and Electoral College uncertainty

A beta-binomial model combines state and district polls with pollster and survey-method effects, then simulates Electoral College outcomes. Historical election anchors support calibration and rolling-origin backtests, keeping the project framed as a transparent research model rather than a betting forecast.

Polling · hierarchical Bayesian model

HUNGARIAN ELECTION — Hungarian polling house effects

Using 120 polls from the 2024–2026 Hungarian political cycle, a hierarchical Dirichlet–multinomial model separates underlying party-preference trends from survey-mode and polling-institute effects. Election results anchor the trend, enabling estimates of systematic house effects and the timing of the shift in political balance.
